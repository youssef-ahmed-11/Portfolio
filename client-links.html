Preview (nothing is saved until you download)

Add a video, strategy, content plan or script link to each client

  1. Choose your website folder (the folder with index.html and content.js). Or choose only your content.js. Your browser may say “Upload”; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  2. Under each client, click + Add a link, pick the type, and paste the link (YouTube, Google Drive, Google Docs, any web link).
  3. Click Download content.js, then upload it to GitHub to replace the content.js that is already there. Netlify publishes it by itself.

Use a computer. Your clients and everything else on the website stay exactly as they are.
