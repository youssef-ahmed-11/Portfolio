How it works

  1. Click the button below and choose your site folder (the one that contains index.html). Your browser may say “Upload”. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; it only lets this page read your folder.
  2. Change text, links, videos, images, clients, and PDFs in the forms. Click Preview website any time to check how it looks.
  3. Click Download updated website at the top. You get yousef-portfolio-netlify.zip, ready to drag onto Netlify. Unzip it first and open index.html to check it.

Use a computer (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox). Phones can’t open folders this way.
